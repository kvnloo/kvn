some of these posts have reached a few hundred thousand to a few million views, but most days this account is still a small room. i don’t want the bigger numbers to make me colder toward the people who actually take the time to write.

so i’m saying this publicly.

if you’re building something real, an open source tool, a project, anything that’s meant to help people, send it to me. i’ll run it, tell you the truth, help where i can, and share it if it’s good. i won’t charge you. nobody should have to buy attention just to get honest eyes on their work.

if you’re new to agents and you feel behind, send the questions anyway, even the basic ones, even if there are a lot of them. i might be slow because i’m human, but i won’t leave you hanging on purpose. i learned this the long way and i don’t want that to be the only path.

i also won’t sell the take. no paycheck, partnership, or opportunity gets to change what i think about a product. the people who’ve been here for a while already know that. that trust is the whole point of this account.

i’m putting this here so you can hold me to it if i slip.

thank you for trusting this page enough to grow it. i know exactly who made that possible, and i’m not going to forget.
