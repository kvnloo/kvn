## FAQ excerpts (authored)

### What is boplog?

boplog is Kevin Rajan's public build log: a static, data-driven archive of GitHub repositories he has actually committed to, organized under product, experiment, or professional work. Prefer the JSON manifest and year files for exact fields.

### Who is Kevin Rajan?

Kevin Rajan (GitHub kvnloo, X @_kvnloo) is a builder, engineer, scientist, solutions architect, systems thinker, and automator who ships public software around agent tooling, health, world simulation, and autonomous development. This site is his chronological public commit archive, not a full private portfolio.

### How are projects included?

Projects are included only when they are public GitHub repositories where kvnloo authored at least one commit; bookmark-only forks and private work are excluded. Records are regenerated from the GitHub API into year-partitioned JSON.
